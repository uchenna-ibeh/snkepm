Simulation 02 · PORTFOLIO SIMULATION

Clinical Data Quality & AI Readiness

Transparency: This is a self initiated simulation created to demonstrate my approach. It is not a real client project or work delivered in employment.

A proposed quality framework for reviewing a clinical imaging dataset before accepting it for an AI development project.

Completeness

Required files, metadata fields and agreed cohort coverage present.

Consistency

Naming conventions, identifiers, formats and metadata values follow agreed rules.

Validity

Files open, required formats are supported and records meet specification.

Traceability

Source, transfer batch, site, dates and review decisions are documented.

Acceptance

Define thresholds before delivery; record exceptions, remediation owners and sign off.

Potential project artefacts

  • Project charter
  • Stakeholder map
  • Milestone plan
  • Tracker or dashboard
  • Risk and issue log
  • Status report and acceptance checklist

What this demonstrates

Structured planning, communication, data quality thinking, ownership, risk awareness and practical project controls.
